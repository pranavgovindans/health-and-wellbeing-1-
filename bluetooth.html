BPM GUIDE

Understanding heart rate

60–100 BPMTypical resting range for most adults.
Below 60 BPMCan be normal for trained athletes; ask a clinician if you feel unwell.
Over 100 BPMCan rise with stress or activity; rest and seek advice for concerning symptoms.
VitaWell is not a medical device. Seek urgent help for chest pain, severe breathlessness, fainting, or sudden symptoms.
